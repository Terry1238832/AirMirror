Privacy Notice简体中文

The App Store edition includes a native Mac receiver and an iPad screen-sharing companion. Wireless video uses an encrypted local connection. Mac also includes USB mirroring and a separate WindowPaste capture-and-copy panel.

Wireless connection

  1. Open AirMirror on both devices and join the same reachable Wi-Fi network.
  2. Choose “Pair iPad” on Mac. Scan the QR code on iPad, or enter the pairing token manually.
  3. Compare the confirmation numbers and allow the connection on Mac.
  4. Choose “Share Screen” on iPad and confirm in the system picker. You can stop at any time.

Mac not found or unable to connect

Check local network permissions, the Mac receiver, and whether Wi-Fi allows devices to reach each other. Guest or school access-point isolation can block wireless sharing. USB does not require the same Wi-Fi.

USB and audio

Use a data-capable cable, unlock your iPhone or iPad and trust the Mac. macOS camera permission is used to read the USB device screen. Audio input permission is needed only for optional USB device sound. Wireless sharing currently sends video only.

WindowPaste

Open the separate auxiliary panel, choose a target, capture and copy, then paste manually. Capturing another app window requires screen recording permission.
